About MindAssist

Your AI-Powered Mental Health Companion — Evidence-based depression risk prediction using machine learning.

📋 Project Overview

MindAssist is an intelligent system designed for early detection of depression risk using machine learning. The system analyzes responses to the PHQ-9 (Patient Health Questionnaire-9) and provides evidence-based risk predictions to help individuals understand their mental health status.

🔍 Early Detection 🤖 AI-Powered 💚 Support & Care
